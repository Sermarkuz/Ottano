# Google Play Store Listing — English

## App name
Ottano

## Short description
Fuel prices, map, routes and refuelling cost simulator for Italy.

## Full description
Ottano turns Italian fuel-price open data into a practical everyday tool.

With Ottano you can:
- compare petrol, diesel, LPG, methane/CNG, HVO and premium fuel prices;
- view fuel stations on a map;
- search around a location or along a route;
- sort by price, distance or estimated real cost;
- simulate refuelling by litres, budget or tank level;
- estimate cost per 100 km and annual fuel spending;
- choose from hundreds of vehicle models and powertrains;
- use device location only when you request location-based features such as “Near me”;
- refresh data from available sources;
- manually load official MIMIT CSV/CSV.GZ files.

Displayed fuel prices come from the Italian Ministry of Enterprises and Made in Italy fuel-price observatory and reflect prices reported by station operators. Actual pump prices may change after the latest report.

Mapping uses OpenStreetMap-based services. Place search and routing can depend on external services and network availability.

Ottano does not sell personal data and does not include its own advertising system.
